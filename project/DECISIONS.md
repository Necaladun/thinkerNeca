# Decisions and working assumptions

> **Status boundary:** Decisions about independent terrain/rendering refer to experimental [PR #4](https://github.com/Necaladun/thinkerNeca/pull/4) until it merges. Design approval does not mean default-branch implementation.

Record choices that materially constrain implementation. **Established** means intentional and current; **Trial** means subject to gameplay testing. A proposal remains in [IDEAS](IDEAS.md), not here, unless adopted. Updated 2026-11-07.

| ID | State | Decision / rationale | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DEC-001 | Established | **Fresh games are the target.** Old saves and legacy scenarios need not remain compatible across mod revisions. New-game same-build save/reload still must work. | A specific compatibility need appears |
| DEC-002 | Established | **Independent minor terrain** has its own type/code per tile; no repurposing native major landmark flags/names to represent Jungle, Uranium or Titanium. | Changing core terrain architecture |
| DEC-003 | Established | **Retire Nessus Canyon**; preserve unused native slot 10 (`LM_RESERVED_10=0x200`) for future major design. The former Canyon generator, bonus and `canyon` setting are gone. | MAP-003 design approved |
| DEC-004 | Established, implementation under test | **Prefer native-stage terrain rendering** to overlays drawn over units/fungus/improvements. Retain experimental fallbacks until verified. | Release/PRACX renderer strategy decided |
| DEC-005 | Established | **No broad AI/terrain rebalance while verifying terrain foundations.** Preserve strong altitude-dependent Solar yields; focus on getting AI to exploit specialised improvements rather than automatically nerfing Solar. | BAL-002/003 or AI-001 advanced |
| DEC-006 | Trial | **3/4/4 nutrient/mineral/energy limits** in the distributed experimental INI; previous trial was 3/3/3 and original cap 2/2/2 remains available. This is a test configuration, not a hardcoded mechanics change. | Gameplay evidence from BAL-001 |
| DEC-007 | Trial / workaround | **`base_capture_fix=0`** because of reported probe-team issues; other capture fixes are lost with it. `probe_action_fix=1` remains enabled. | BUG-001 isolated/fixed |
| DEC-008 | Current play preference | **No polar caps; exclude Sunspots and Base Asteroid Strike; extra starting units.** Test-profile values are in [configuration](technical/configuration.md). Not a universal balance standard. | Player preference changes |
| DEC-009 | Established, user-verified | **Sunny Mesa adds +1 energy only in its central 3×3**, not the wider 7×7. Both actual and projected energy use tile codes 0–8; user confirmed this effect in-game. | Future landmark balance decision |
| DEC-010 | Rejected / reverted | **Do not raise Mesa's 3×3 centre to ~2,000 m with the existing renderer.** The stepped variant produced a rounded/bulging summit instead of a flat top in the 2026-11-04 user screenshot. Restore the 5×5 1,000–1,999 m plateau and surrounding sub-1,000 m rim; keep core +1E. | A different rendering/terrain solution is explicitly proposed |
| DEC-011 | Established for supported Develop variant 0 | **Custom minor resource art uses a local indexed `neca_sprites.pcx` and native-stage Sprite-pointer substitution.** User confirmed actual blue-grey Titanium, transparency, zoom and +1M. Keep native draw geometry, layering and fallback, not a hand-positioned overlay. This decision does not assert variant 1, Release/PRACX or arbitrary-size art support. | Additional drawing contexts or different sprite formats become necessary |
| DEC-012 | Established combat-design policy; source bug outstanding | **Keep `ignore_reactor_power=1` as the intended balance rule.** Reactor upgrades retain construction-cost benefits but must not increase *effective* HP or endurance, including against ordinary bombardment. The engine retains `10 × reactor level` stored HP and normalizes direct-combat/duel damage; ordinary bombardment currently fails to normalize (BUG-004). Any future chassis HP bonus must be reactor-independent in effective terms. | Only if the fundamental reactor-combat policy is deliberately reconsidered |
| DEC-013 | Established design, implementation pending | **New optional telemetry is separate from legacy `debug.txt` and does not alter gameplay or RNG.** Store campaign-specific snapshots, event history, AI decision evidence, manifests and session/save lineage. Ordinary playable games and AI simulations use the same recorder. | Proven implementation constraint or reproducibility issue |
| DEC-014 | Established design, implementation pending | **AI-only automation should reuse ordinary single-player turn processing and the existing Scenario Editor AI handover path first.** Suppress or resolve only classified blocking interactions; keep native save/reload and victory behaviour correct, support unattended windowed games, and postpone true headless operation. | Source/runtime shows a safer mechanism or a necessary behavioural trade-off |
| DEC-015 | Established; supported Develop path user-verified | **Render independent Uranium/Titanium geological art at the native Uranium-style underlay stage**, beneath ordinary N/M/E resource icons and before unit/base foregrounds; preserve clipping, fog, yields and Inspector. The original editor-painted `LM_URANIUM` + nutrient resource tile (47,67) is a concrete *visual reference*, not evidence of a validated hook. Simultaneous Uranium+Titanium is **not generated naturally**; forced overlap requires a safe, predictable draw/fallback, but the two geological sprites need not both be legible (overdraw/artifact is accepted). REND-005's underlay is implemented on draft PR #4 and user-verified in Windows Develop with distinct blue-grey Titanium beneath ordinary resource art, including fog/zoom, unit/base and reload smoke. User deferred dedicated missing-art fallback and native Scenario Editor Uranium compatibility tests; those are not merge blockers. Constructed overlap safety remains TERR-004, and Release/PRACX parity remains separately tracked. Do not substitute native LM flags for persistent minor storage. | Native draw investigation requires an explicitly justified alternative composition stage |

| DEC-016 | Established; v3 data model implemented, constructed-stack runtime tests pending | **Retain all 16 native `LM_*` flag identities and the separate `THMIN001` minor-terrain system; extend minor storage to support multiple feature types on one tile with per-type codes.** Minor feature bonuses stack according to their existing resource/yield branches and caps; overlapping native landmark flags and minor features should be representable without silently deleting one another. **Generation policy:** maintain exclusion among current Jungle, Uranium and Titanium so they never naturally stack (also retain current no-overlap-with-major rules); future types may explicitly opt into stacking following balance/design review. Keep generation policy distinct from storage capability, and handle render fallback separately. This supersedes the former single-type-per-tile limitation and is implemented as ordered per-tile `(type,code)` pairs in `THMIN001` v3, with synthetic stacking/save parser tests passed. Deliberately constructed multi-type tile semantics/drawing remain unverified in Windows and their tests are **user-deferred, not PR #4 merge blockers** because ordinary world generation excludes these overlaps. Reopen them before a later feature/editor intentionally creates stacks. The native Canyon slot `0x200` remains reserved. | A demonstrated technical incompatibility or a new design decision |

| DEC-017 | Approved balance trial; qualitative Windows multi-seed reports positive, quantitative balance deferred | **Disperse geological deposits without increasing expected tile coverage:** shared Uranium/Titanium generation targets 3–5 tiles (weighted 1:3:1; mean 4), ~50% more deposits than the old target (3–9 per type vs 2–6), retains the existing two-tile buffer and lowland bias, and **preserves fungus** rather than clearing it. This makes fungus-covered geology a future terraforming opportunity, as the current fungus yield branches suppress the underlying geological yield until clearing. Failed placement attempts may reduce realised count; do not force fill. No INI, saves or rendering changes. Status trial until representative fresh maps are checked (TERR-002). | New-map seed distributions/early-game balance justify different coverage or type policies |

| DEC-018 | Approved trial; targeted Windows checks positive | **Independent Uranium and Titanium do not benefit Forests.** Exclude +1 geological mineral/energy bonuses from actual and projected Forest yield branches, preserving ordinary Forest yields and Jungle nutrients; the stored geology remains and may be exploited after removing the Forest. **Newly generated Titanium avoids ordinary mineral-resource squares** (both explicit and procedural; use actual resource classification), but normal nutrient/energy resources may overlap it. **If a Unity pod resource discovery would add a normal mineral resource to an independent Titanium tile, reroll that resource to nutrient or energy with equal probability.** Use the established explicit bonus-resolution flags so coordinates cannot override the reroll; keep the resource reward, Uranium+energy, other rewards and original native landmarks untouched. Existing saves and native landmark mechanics remain unchanged. **User confirmed 7 November:** retain the existing single extra Titanium Mine modifier for the current small-patch trial to encourage Mines; this remains provisional rather than permanent. Windows user observations: Forest suppression and restoration on both geological types, Titanium Mine+Road 3M Flat/4M Rolling, fungus clearing, save/reload/turn advance, natural nutrient/energy overlaps on Titanium and no mineral-resource overlaps in inspected fresh maps. A systematic multi-seed survey remains open. AI-002 now has a scoped non-rocky automatic Mine implementation in source/CI; behavioural acceptance is deferred to longer logged games. | Actual Windows new-map/forest/AI forecast tests or the small-patch Mine economics warrant revision |

| DEC-019 | Approved scoped AI trial; extended runtime validation user-deferred | **Automatic Former Mines may exploit independent Titanium on Rolling or Flat land without changing ordinary non-rocky Mine eligibility.** Mine+Road must increase minerals. Flat must retain at least 1 nutrient after the actual conditional Mine penalty, and established nutrient improvements on nonrocky Titanium must be protected. Compare projected Mine output against Forest/Fungus/Borehole, eligible Solar and Flat Farm yields, with only a modest +4 priority for eligible Titanium. The experimental caps are 3/4/4 N/M/E; preserve the existing Titanium Mine enhancement and do not alter player-directed Mine/Road. Source is implemented in draft PR #4 under AI-002 with Develop/Release CI passed. **Windows AI runtime remains untested; user explicitly deferred AI-002 behaviour/balance validation until multiple longer instrumented AI-only games can be run (TEL-001/TEL-002 and AUTO-001/002). This is not a PR #4 merge blocker, and deferral is not acceptance.** | Runtime evidence shows poor choices, or balance/caps change |

**Not decided:** reducing Forest to 1/1/1; making geological bonuses Mine-only; a replacement for major slot 10; reworking Former scoring. Those are tracked as TBD in [WORK-QUEUE](WORK-QUEUE.md) or exploratory [IDEAS](IDEAS.md).

**Versioning:** PR #3 merged the type/code foundation; draft PR #4 carries the present generation, native rendering, slot-10 and test-INI changes. See [Fork changelog](../Fork-Changelog.md). Git is the precise implementation history, not these summaries.
